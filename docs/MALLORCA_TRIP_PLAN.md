## Day-by-day itinerary

### Friday 16 October — airport, Cala Deià, Sóller, Alcúdia
1. Arrive at Palma Airport and collect the Wiber car.
2. Drive to **Cala Deià** for a short beach/cove stop if sea conditions and weather allow.
3. Lunch at **Ca’s Patró March**, a seafood restaurant above the water. Reservation and opening hours need to be confirmed.
   - Reservations: https://caspatromarch.myrestoo.net/
4. Short walk through **Deià village**.
5. Continue to **Sóller** and, if time permits, take the historic tram to **Port de Sóller**. Check the official timetable before relying on this.
   - Timetable: https://trendesoller.com/eng/timetable
6. Drive back to Inmood Aucanada in Alcúdia.

This is an ambitious day after the flight. If the rental pickup is delayed, shorten Sóller/Port de Sóller rather than rushing the drive. Confirm the actual flight arrival time and restaurant availability.

### Saturday 17 October — winery, small village stop, horse riding
1. Leave Alcúdia with enough time to reach Binissalem before the 12:00 winery booking.
2. **12:00 — Bodegas José L. Ferrer**.
3. If time allows, make a short 20–30 minute stop at **Biniagual** for stone houses and vineyards, or walk around Binissalem.
4. **Rancho Can Picafort** for the planned one-hour forest horse ride. The exact riding time must be confirmed before locking the order of stops.
5. Return to Alcúdia for the evening.

Biniagual map: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Biniagual+Mallorca

### Sunday 18 October — Alcúdia market, caves, Santanyí, Palma
1. Check out of Inmood Aucanada and visit the **Alcúdia Sunday market** in the historic centre (generally listed around 08:00–13:30).
   - Map: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Alcudia+Sunday+Market
2. Drive to **Cuevas del Drach** for the pre-booked entry time.
3. Continue to **Santanyí** for a short walk and food only if the schedule and weather permit.
4. Check in at **Hotel Las Arenas**, Can Pastilla (check-in from 14:00).
5. Spend the evening in Palma's old town or along the waterfront.

If the weather is very poor or time is tight after the caves, skip Santanyí and drive directly to the hotel/Palma.

### Monday 19 October — Palma and flight home
1. Short morning walk and coffee in Palma.
2. Check out of Hotel Las Arenas by 12:00.
3. Return the Wiber car at 13:00 and take the shuttle to the airport.
4. Wizz Air flight W9 5352 departs PMI at 15:55 for London Luton.
